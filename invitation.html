the invitation

From your home in Miami to our workshop in Italy, and back. Nine steps, and one person who follows you through all of them.

in italy, guests are welcomed at home.

So we invite you to our home, in Milan, to choose yours. You see where it is made, you hold the materials, and you meet the hands that will make it.

  1. I
    miami

    you ask for an invitation

    A few lines about your home. We reply ourselves, not a machine.

  2. II
    miami

    we meet you in miami

    One of us sees the space with you, takes the measures and draws a first idea. who meets the client in Miami

  3. III
    miami

    the envelope arrives

    Sealed in wax: your dates, your flight, who will be waiting for you. who sends it, and when

  4. IV
    miami → milano

    your flight from miami

    Booked by us. who the trip is for, how many people

  5. V
    milano

    a car at arrivals

    A driver takes you straight into the city.

  6. VI
    milano

    the showroom, just for you

    A private visit. You hold the stone, the wood, the metal. You meet the people who will make your furniture, and decide with them.

    Showroom: [address], milano · workshop: [town, region]

  7. VII
    milano

    some nights in miami

    Time for the city, too. hotel

  8. VIII
    milano → the workshop

    your furniture is made

    Back home, you receive photographs from the workshop as it takes shape. production time

  9. IX
    miami

    delivered and installed in miami

    We take care of the building's rules and the insurance certificates. Your certificate and the samples of your materials arrive with it. who installs in Miami
