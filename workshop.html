one family, one name.

morzanti

Mornati · Zani · Zanti

say it: mor-ZAN-tee

An Italian family is never small: everyone has a place at the table and a hand in the work. morzanti is a family like that, one name for all of us, with a chair kept for you.

our mark, inside every drawer.

Italian goldsmiths sign their work with a punch: a shape, a number and the letters of their city. We sign ours the same way. Open the first drawer of any piece and you will find our mark, burned into the wood, next to its number.

The same number is engraved on the brass plate inside the first door, and written on your certificate of origin and warranty.
